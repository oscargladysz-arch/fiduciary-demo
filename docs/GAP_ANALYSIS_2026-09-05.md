## 1. Bottom line

Round 1 was executed. ### 4.2 Engine design and correctness

Benchmark engine (`src/tark_benchmark.py`, `docs/benchmark_methodology.md`):

11. Category error in the statistic. `ks_pme(flows, levels)` (`src/tark_analytics.py:169-180`) is fed a ladder of an equal-weighted average of appraised NAVs (`src/tark_benchmark.py:523-528, 550-551`) and the result is stored and displayed as `ks_pme`. The card's gloss says "Did the fund beat simply buying an index with the same cash, at the same times? (Kaplan-Schoar Public Market Equivalent)" (`src/build_site.py:43-44`). The composite is not a market, cannot be bought, and is smoothed the same way the fund is. This appears on 9 cards, in 36 memos, in cells 1.8 and 5.5, and in the Screener column. With two-point flows the number is a cumulative growth ratio and should be named as one ("relative wealth ratio vs peer average").
12. Factor (k) is filled with the (g)/(h) comparison. The rule's benchmark factor and its "reasonable number of similar investment alternatives" language are different requirements. Cells 5.1 to 5.7 render under paragraph (k) (`src/tark_data.py:91`), the factor-5 typed fact is the composite selection, and cell 1.8 under (g) restates the same composite number. The methodology's own mapping (`docs/benchmark_methodology.md:293-296`) says Lane C answers a different requirement from (k), yet Lane C is the (k) primary for nine products. The only third-party comparators (BKLN, PSP, VNQ) are demoted to secondary.
13. The composite does not deserve 10/12. Alignment is by the four-digit year of each member's fiscal year end (`:186, :355`). For Cliffwater (March year end since 2022) the fund's FY2023 (April 2022 to March 2023) is averaged against peers' calendar 2023 and pflex's July 2022 to June 2023. The composite's 2022 row (−0.95%, the rate-shock year) is dropped because the fund's 2022 is a three-month stub, while nine months of 2022 remain inside the fund's FY2023. pflex's FY2021 (July 2020 to June 2021, +28.02%, the COVID rebound) lifts the composite's 2021 row from 8.6% (December-fiscal-year members) to 13.46%. Recomputed on calendar 2021 to 2025 with data on disk: fund 1.5567 vs December-fiscal-year composite 1.5143, ratio 1.028 (1.026 with pflex as filed), against the published 0.9756. The flagship "lagged" verdict flips. Three evergreen-PE composites open on a two-fund year (rows need only two members, `:232`, while the candidate is described as a 3-plus-member cohort, `:218`). The rubric awards strategy 3 by plurality (2 of 4 members share the sub-strategy, `:214-215`) and investability 2 to a construct that cannot be invested in.
14. Published indices are structurally excluded. `investability` and `data_quality` together remove 4 of 12 points from any cited or licensed index (`:323-325, :335-336`). There is no way to load a licensed or published series (`CANDIDATES` is code, `:93-149`). Headline quarterly CDLI and NFI-ODCE returns are published by their sponsors, so "not held" is a choice.

PSP grew 4.2% over 2021 to 2026 (`data/series/psp.csv`), which is why hl_paf shows 1.9565 against it.
